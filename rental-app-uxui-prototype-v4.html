Five semantic families, each a tint paired with a matching ink: settled for money received and rules passed, attention for a limit approaching, signal for a legal or financial breach, progress for work in hand, and neutral for anything that merely informs. Ratios are body text measured on the surface value; a dash means the token is a surface, not a text colour. Every colour is paired with a word, so status is never carried by hue alone.

Design noteOne blueprint ramp carries all structure — headings, primary actions, drawing lines. Four semantic families sit on top of it for state, plus a fifth for work in hand, so no screen needs a colour that is not on this sheet. The old tertiary grey measured 2.8:1 against the card; it is now 5.7:1, which is why captions, placeholders and frame notes are readable at last.
